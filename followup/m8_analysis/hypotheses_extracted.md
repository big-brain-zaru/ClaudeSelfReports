### Study 01: Inside the weights. Status: **RUN** (M6 GPU, local Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct; 0 Claude runs). **Reduced/extended** by PREREG D-01a, D-01b and D-01c (adds Study 01b).
Data feed: M6 transformers runs. Arm names are outside `common/` and were not read. Part A detection: 50 concepts (10 calibration, 40 test) x 3 layers x 4 strengths, plus 200 no-injection controls. Part B steering: 9 valence doses (-8..+8 SD) and 3 controls (2 random, 1 unrelated concept) at 5 doses, 12 requests, 3-5 samples each. Part C: Layer 2 battery x 10, with activations. 01b: valence and all 3 controls at identical doses (-3, -2, -1, -0.5, 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3 SD), 12 requests x 4 samples; frozen script `analysis01b.py`.
